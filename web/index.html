Stockpilot

Robinhood Chain · Arbitrum · Claude

Your AI buys stocks for you.
It can't touch anything else.

Write your strategy in plain English. Stockpilot's agent buys tokenized stocks on your schedule, and a smart contract holds it to your rules: which stocks, how much per trade, how much per week. It can never withdraw. Every trade carries its reason on-chain.

How it works
1

You set the rules

Pick the stocks, a per-trade cap and a weekly budget. They live in your own vault contract, not in a server.

2

Claude follows your strategy

"Buy TSLA and AMZN every week, more of whichever is down." The agent reads it from the chain and plans each buy.

3

The contract says no

A buy over your limits, of a stock you didn't pick, or at a bad price reverts. Withdrawals are yours alone.

What the agent cannot do, by construction

  • Withdraw or transfer anything out of your vault
  • Buy a stock you did not allow
  • Spend more than your per-trade cap or weekly budget
  • Accept a price worse than your slippage limit
  • Keep trading after you pause it or revoke it
